How to read this. One retailer's wording is not all of coffee: "no match" means not in this set of reviews, which is different from "never said." A note with no earlier match does not show that anything was added to a coffee; new varieties and processing methods can create new flavors. Nothing you type leaves your browser.

About the data

Source: reviews from Sweet Maria's archive pages (crop years 2008–2011), read through the Internet Archive. Counts are the number of reviews that mention a descriptor. Labels: common = in at least 5% of reviews; rare = in fewer; no match = in none.
